## en

- **Drivers from anywhere with the desktop app.** Online leagues no longer need everyone on one WiFi. On the Drivers page, sign in with your FRLcast website account and link your event: drivers type its code in the driver app from home, their sign-ins appear on the Race page to accept, and flags, positions and penalties reach their phones. Team radio from their phones comes back to your console and overlays.
- You keep everything the desktop app has (Piper voice, timing API, driver edits) while drivers connect over the internet.
- A sign-in that arrives while you are on another page now shows up as soon as you open the Race page.

## id

- **Pembalap dari mana saja dengan aplikasi desktop.** Liga online tidak perlu lagi satu WiFi. Di halaman Pembalap, masuk dengan akun website FRLcast lalu hubungkan event-mu: pembalap mengetik kodenya di aplikasi driver dari rumah, pendaftaran mereka muncul di halaman Race untuk diterima, dan bendera, posisi, serta penalti sampai ke HP mereka. Radio tim dari HP mereka masuk ke console dan overlay-mu.
- Semua fitur aplikasi desktop tetap ada (suara Piper, timing API, edit pembalap) sementara pembalap tersambung lewat internet.
- Pendaftaran yang masuk saat kamu sedang di halaman lain sekarang langsung terlihat begitu halaman Race dibuka.
