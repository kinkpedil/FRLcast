## en
- Tidier console: Event scenes, Vision/AI, Overlays and Layout are rearranged so both columns are balanced and nothing is cut off.
- Event scenes now lists every widget in grouped columns, including ticker, fastest lap, sectors, radio, poll, sponsor, countdown and QR, which could not be switched there before.
