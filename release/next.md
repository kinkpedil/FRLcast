## en
- Drift rounds: a drift round runs practice then the drift competition, and finishing it scores the round in the championship from the bracket result, with a pole bonus for the top qualifier.
- Judges score qualifying runs on Line, Angle and Style and can judge from their phones with their own link: battle calls with big buttons, scores with sliders.
- New drift overlays: the qualifying run score reveal with Line/Angle/Style bars, a qualifying board, and battle calls revealed one by one. Optional battle for third place.
- Driver app: shows on track, lead or chase, up next and the battle result, and the voice spotter calls them out.

## id
- Ronde drift: ronde drift berisi latihan lalu kompetisi drift, dan saat diselesaikan poin ronde langsung masuk championship dari hasil bracket, dengan bonus pole untuk top qualifier.
- Juri menilai run kualifikasi per Line, Angle, dan Style, dan bisa menilai dari HP lewat link masing-masing: vote battle dengan tombol besar, nilai dengan slider.
- Overlay drift baru: pengumuman skor run kualifikasi dengan bar Line/Angle/Style, papan kualifikasi, dan vote battle yang dibuka satu per satu. Battle perebutan juara 3 opsional.
- Aplikasi driver: menampilkan giliran di lintasan, lead atau chase, giliran berikutnya, dan hasil battle, lalu spotter suara membacakannya.
