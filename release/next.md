## en

- Start lights: step a five-light gantry from the Race page. It shows on its own overlay and on the drivers' phones.
- Race control can send a short note to one driver's phone, and the phone shows a drive-through serve countdown.
- Commentary now calls the white flag and the pit lane opening or closing.
- Multi-class fields now keep their classes on a hosted event, so the WEC and IMSA skins group by class online too.
- Results overlay shows how many places each driver gained from the grid.
- Audience prediction game at /predict: the crowd picks the winner and sees who called it at the flag.

## id

- Start lights: atur gantry lima lampu dari halaman Race. Muncul di overlay sendiri dan di HP driver.
- Race control bisa kirim catatan singkat ke satu HP driver, dan HP menampilkan hitung mundur serve drive-through.
- Commentary kini menyebut bendera putih dan pit lane buka atau tutup.
- Kelas mobil (multi-class) kini tersimpan di event hosted, jadi skin WEC dan IMSA mengelompokkan per kelas secara online juga.
- Overlay hasil menampilkan berapa posisi naik tiap driver dari grid.
- Game tebak pemenang di /predict: penonton pilih pemenang dan lihat siapa yang benar saat finish.
