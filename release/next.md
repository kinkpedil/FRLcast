## en
- Licence points: every decided penalty also costs points on the driver's licence. Reach the limit and the driver sits out the next round. Turn it on from the Championship page; drivers see their count in the app.
- Voice spotter in the driver app: flags, penalties, race-control notes and team radio are said out loud, so drivers do not have to look at the phone mid-corner.
- The race report now charts every lap time and ranks drivers by consistency.

## id
- Poin lisensi: tiap penalti yang diputuskan juga mengurangi poin lisensi pembalap. Tembus batas, pembalap absen satu ronde. Aktifkan dari halaman Championship; pembalap melihat poinnya di aplikasi.
- Spotter suara di aplikasi driver: bendera, penalti, pesan race control, dan radio tim dibacakan, jadi pembalap tidak perlu melirik HP di tengah tikungan.
- Laporan balapan kini menampilkan grafik semua waktu lap dan peringkat konsistensi pembalap.
