## en

- **Knockout qualifying (optional)**: run Q1, Q2 and Q3 with your own cut sizes. Knocked-out cars keep their place and time, and the tower shows the elimination line.
- **Battles on air by themselves**: with Auto-director on, a fight within a second puts the head-to-head on screen and takes it down when it is over.
- **Positions lap by lap** chart in the race report.
- **Discord posts that fit the session**: best-lap table for qualifying, full result for a race, and the standings when a round is scored. Buttons to post any time.
- **Driver app update**: a pit board on the floating window (place, gap ahead and behind, laps left) and a Report button that sends incidents to a new Incident reports queue in race control.
- **Entry page** (`/join?event=CODE`): drivers enter before race day, everyone sees the entry list, and they check in on the day.
- Online events need a one-time database update for reports, entries and check-in; until then everything else keeps working.

## id

- **Kualifikasi knockout (opsional)**: jalankan Q1, Q2, dan Q3 dengan jumlah lolos yang kamu tentukan. Mobil yang tersingkir tetap memegang posisi dan waktunya, dan tower menampilkan garis eliminasi.
- **Duel tayang otomatis**: dengan Auto-director aktif, duel dalam jarak satu detik menayangkan head-to-head dan menurunkannya setelah selesai.
- **Grafik posisi per lap** di laporan race.
- **Postingan Discord sesuai sesi**: tabel best lap untuk kualifikasi, hasil lengkap untuk race, dan klasemen saat ronde dicatat. Ada tombol untuk mengirim kapan saja.
- **Update aplikasi driver**: pit board di jendela mengambang (posisi, gap depan dan belakang, sisa lap) dan tombol Lapor yang mengirim insiden ke antrean Laporan insiden baru di race control.
- **Halaman pendaftaran** (`/join?event=KODE`): pembalap mendaftar sebelum hari race, semua orang melihat daftar peserta, dan check-in di hari H.
- Event online perlu satu kali update database untuk laporan, pendaftaran, dan check-in; sampai itu dijalankan, fitur lain tetap berjalan.
