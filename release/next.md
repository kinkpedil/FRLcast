## en
- New Design studio on the Layout page: make the broadcast look like anything, from clean to completely absurd.
- Nine ready-made wild presets: Comic book, Vaporwave, 8-bit arcade, Brutalist, Sketchbook, Glitch, Terminal, Race livery and Kawaii.
- Change every colour, pick fonts for text, numbers and titles (Windows, Google Fonts or your own font file), and put a picture behind every panel.
- Upload your own pictures and fonts, place stickers anywhere on screen, and write free CSS for anything else.
- Export a look as one file and import it in another league, files included.

## id
