## en

- White flag: raise it from race control, and it shows on the overlay and on every driver's phone.
- Pit lane open or close: one button, a PITS CLOSED badge on the overlay and a note on the phone.
- Blue flag now reaches a backmarker the leader has lapped, even on the timing-API feed.
- Endurance races show the time remaining on the driver's phone, not a lap count.

## id

- Bendera putih: kibarkan dari race control, muncul di overlay dan di HP tiap driver.
- Buka atau tutup pit lane: satu tombol, badge PITS CLOSED di overlay dan info di HP.
- Bendera biru kini sampai ke driver yang sudah di-lap pemimpin, walau lewat feed timing API.
- Balapan Endurance menampilkan sisa waktu di HP driver, bukan hitungan lap.
