## en

- Penalty appeals: a driver can appeal a penalty from the app. It reaches the new Penalty appeals card in race control, where a steward upholds it (the penalty stands) or overturns it (the penalty is dropped) with a ruling.

## id

- Banding penalti: driver bisa mengajukan banding penalti dari aplikasi. Bandingnya masuk ke kartu Penalty appeals di race control, tempat steward menguatkan (penalti tetap) atau membatalkan (penalti dihapus) dengan sebuah putusan.
