What's new

The console keeps getting better. The latest additions:

  • Team radio, on airA driver types a call (like BOX BOX) in the app, it reaches every teammate's floating window, shows on the broadcast, and is read aloud, then clears itself after 10 to 20s.
  • Eight more skinsDTM, Formula E, GTWC, IMSA, IndyCar, NASCAR, Porsche Cup and Super GT, eleven looks in all.
  • Scene stingersAn accent-colour wipe with your wordmark plays when you switch event scenes, only on scenes, never on a flag.
  • Race recap to DiscordA written summary of the race, built for you and posted to Discord in one click.
  • More broadcast toolsHighlight-reel marker export, a multiview control room, and saved "looks" that store a skin + theme you can reapply.
  • Self-updating overlayThe overlay heals its own connection and picks up each new version on its own, no more refreshing the OBS source after an update.
  • Broadcast skinsMotoGP, WEC and F1 on top of Classic, class grouping, 3-letter codes, team-colour tabs and a custom header wordmark. A skin changes shape; the theme still sets colours.
  • New overlay widgetsFastest-lap banner, sector-time card, team radio, audience poll, sponsor rotator, pre-show countdown and a driver intro card, each a toggle you can place on the layout.
  • Manual modeFor a low-spec machine with no camera: set the order by drag or ▲▼, add laps by hand, by typed time, or with a per-driver stopwatch.
  • Public live pageA no-login page viewers follow on their phone, classification, flag, fastest lap and championship, plus live audience voting.
  • Shortcuts & Stream DeckBind any control to a key you choose; a Stream Deck Hotkey button sends the same keystroke, no plugin, no server.
  • OBS WebSocketOptional: let the console cut OBS scenes on a flag and save a replay clip on a marker.
  • Faster operatingOne-click scene bar, an auto-director for the fastest-lap banner and results, and undo with Ctrl+Z.
  • Bilingual manualThe in-console Help now covers every feature in both English and Indonesian.

Every desktop version, with what changed

Two people use this, and they use it differently.

RACE CONTROL
  1. Sign in and open your eventYour event is yours. Two leagues running the same night never see each other's timing.
  2. Build the gridAdd drivers yourself, or accept the ones who sign in from the app. Accepting one puts them on the grid.
  3. Point OBS at the overlayOne browser source per overlay, or one scene source for all of them.
  4. Run the sessionStart the race, work the flags, hand out penalties. The drivers see it on their phones.

DRIVERS
  1. Install the appOne Android file, about 33 KB. No store account needed.
  2. Register onceRacing name, number and a password. Race control decides who gets in.
  3. Sign in to the eventThe server address your race control gives you, your number, your password.
  4. Turn on the floating flagIt rides over FR Legends while you drive, at whatever size you drag it to.
Get the driver app

The flag reaches the driver, not just the stream.

A phone in a pocket is no use mid corner, so the app draws its own window on top of the game. Flags land there in about a second. Penalties arrive with the steward's reason attached, in the same words race control typed.

Download the driver app APK · 33 KB · Android 7.0 and up
  • Floating windowDrag it anywhere, drag the corner to any size down to a thumbnail. It stays where you put it.
  • PenaltiesA banner with what you were given and why, then the window goes back to the flag.
